Account deletion

Delete your OweMate account

You can request permanent deletion of your OweMate account and associated app data either inside the app or from the web.

Option 1 — Delete from the app

Open OweMate, go to the account/settings area, choose Delete account, review the deletion information, confirm that you understand the action is permanent, and complete the final confirmation.

Option 2 — Request deletion from the web

If you no longer have access to the app, email privacy@owemate.net from the email address associated with your OweMate account. Use the subject OweMate Account Deletion Request.

Please include only the information needed to identify your OweMate account. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, PINs or other authentication secrets.

What is deleted

For an eligible single-user space, account deletion removes the OweMate sign-in account and associated personal-space data, including records, people, settings, related application data and OweMate-hosted attachments.

Shared Space accounts

If your current OweMate space has another member, deletion is blocked until the sharing relationship is resolved. This protects the other member's shared data from being silently destroyed. Contact support@owemate.net if you need help resolving the Shared Space before deletion.

Store purchase history

Deleting your OweMate account does not delete purchase records independently maintained by Google Play or Apple under their own policies.
