Privacy without theatre

Security you can explain.

OweMate separates ordinary shared records from private Vault content and gives users explicit control over recovery, sharing and deletion.

Secure Vault

Encrypt before upload.

Secure Vault notes and files are encrypted on your device before they are sent to OweMate. Shared-space membership alone does not grant Vault access.

  • Private secure notes
  • Encrypted file storage
  • Dedicated recovery code
◇
Protected access

Explicit, read-only and delayed.

A trusted partner can request read-only Vault access only after the owner enables it. Requests are protected by a server-side 30-day window, and the owner can deny or revoke access.

  • 30-day protection window
  • Owner can deny or revoke
  • No create, edit, upload or delete rights

Recovery

Your recovery code matters.

Restore an existing Vault on another device using its recovery code. Recovery codes can be rotated without changing the Vault encryption key or secure content.

  • Cross-device Vault recovery
  • Recovery-code rotation

Questions, answered

Know exactly what you’re getting.

OweMate is intentionally straightforward about the product, the price and the boundaries of its privacy features.

Is OweMate a subscription?

No. Premium Lifetime is US$4.99 as a one-time purchase. The free plan supports up to 5 active records in a personal space.

Can two people use the same OweMate records?

Yes. Premium unlocks one Shared Space for two people. Both members can access the records and people stored in that shared space.

Is Secure Vault the same as Shared Space?

No. Vault content is private to the account. Shared-space membership alone does not grant access to another user’s Vault.

How does Emergency Access work?

The Vault owner must enable it for the trusted partner. A request starts a server-side 30-day protection window. The owner can deny or revoke the request, and released access is read-only.

Can I delete my OweMate account?

Yes. OweMate has an in-app deletion flow and a web request path. If your current space has another member, the sharing relationship must be resolved first.

Is OweMate available on iPhone?

The iOS app is currently going through rollout and testing. The public App Store release will be linked here when available.
