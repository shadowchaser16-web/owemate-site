Privacy Policy

This policy explains how OweMate: IOU & Debt Tracker accesses, uses, stores, protects and shares information when you use the app.

Effective date: 17 September 2026

1. Scope

OweMate is a personal IOU, lending and borrowing record app. This Privacy Policy applies to the OweMate mobile app and related OweMate services.

2. Information OweMate processes

Account and authentication information

OweMate processes information needed to create and operate an account, including your email address, user and account identifiers, authentication state, account settings and space-membership information.

IOU and Shared Space content

OweMate processes the information you choose to enter, including lending or borrowing records, amounts, dates, notes, people or counterparty information, and Shared Space membership and invitation information. If you join a Shared Space, both members can access the records and people stored in that shared space.

Attachments and files

If you choose to attach a file or image to a record, OweMate stores that attachment in private application storage and associates access with the relevant OweMate space and record.

Secure Vault

Secure Vault notes and files are encrypted on the device before they are sent to OweMate. OweMate also processes technical metadata needed to operate Vault encryption, recovery, emergency-access and access-history features. Secure Vault is not intended to be used as a password manager, and users should not store passwords, PINs, one-time authentication codes or recovery seeds in it.

Notifications

If notifications are enabled, OweMate processes device push-notification registration tokens and related delivery information so it can deliver account, sharing, reminder and security-related notifications.

Purchases and Premium entitlement

When you purchase or restore Premium through Google Play, OweMate processes the product identifier, purchase token and verification or acknowledgement status needed to verify the purchase and activate the Premium entitlement. Payment-card details are processed by Google Play and are not collected by OweMate.

Operational and security information

OweMate and its service providers may process limited technical records such as request timestamps, delivery status, error information and security events when needed to operate, protect or troubleshoot the service.

3. How information is used

  • To create and authenticate accounts.
  • To store, synchronize and display IOU records and other content you choose to enter.
  • To operate Shared Spaces and invitations.
  • To store and retrieve attachments.
  • To operate Secure Vault, recovery and emergency-access features.
  • To send push notifications and transactional emails.
  • To verify purchases, restore Premium and maintain entitlement status.
  • To prevent abuse, investigate failures, secure the service and provide support.

OweMate does not sell personal data and does not use your IOU or Secure Vault content for advertising.

4. Service providers and sharing

OweMate uses service providers to operate the app. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Supabase for authentication, databases, server functions and private file storage.
  • Google Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging for push-notification delivery.
  • Google Play for Android purchases, restoration and billing verification.
  • Brevo for transactional email delivery, including invitations and account or security-related messages.

Information may also be shared with another OweMate user when you explicitly create or join a Shared Space with that person. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

5. Security

OweMate uses authenticated accounts, server-side access controls and private storage rules to restrict access to application data. Network communications use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Secure Vault content is encrypted on the device before upload. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

OweMate retains account and application data while the account is active and as needed to provide the requested features. OweMate provides an in-app account-deletion flow. For an eligible single-user space, deletion permanently removes the OweMate sign-in account, personal-space records, people, settings, related app data and OweMate-hosted attachments.

If your current OweMate space has another member, the sharing relationship must first be resolved so that another person's shared data is not silently destroyed. OweMate support is available to help with this process.

Google Play or Apple may separately retain store purchase history under their own policies. Deleting an OweMate account does not erase records maintained independently by an app store.

Want to delete your OweMate account?
Use the in-app Delete account option or visit OweMate Account Deletion to request deletion from the web.

7. Your choices

You can edit or delete many records inside the app, manage Shared Space participation, control notification permissions through your device settings, and request permanent account deletion.

8. Children's privacy

OweMate is not designed as a service directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through OweMate, contact us so we can review and address the request.

9. Contact

Privacy: privacy@owemate.net
Support: support@owemate.net

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when OweMate features or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
