ACCOUNT CONTROL

Delete your OweMate account

You can request permanent deletion of your OweMate account and associated OweMate data from inside the app or by email if you no longer have app access.

Deletion routesInside OweMateFrom the webWhat is deletedShared Space

Option 1 — Delete inside OweMate

Open OweMate, go to the account/settings area, choose Delete account, review the deletion information and complete the final confirmation. Account deletion is permanent.

Option 2 — Request deletion from the web

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@owemate.net from the email address associated with your OweMate account. Use the subject OweMate Account Deletion Request.

Protect your account

Include only the information needed to identify your OweMate account. Never send your password, Vault recovery code, PIN or one-time authentication code.

What is deleted

For an eligible single-user space, deletion removes the OweMate sign-in account and associated personal-space app data, including records, people, settings, related application data and OweMate-hosted attachments.

Shared Space safeguard

If your current OweMate space still has another member, deletion is blocked until the sharing relationship is resolved. This protects the other member's shared data from being silently destroyed. Contact support@owemate.net if you need help resolving the Shared Space first.

App-store purchase history

Deleting your OweMate account does not delete purchase records independently maintained by Google Play or Apple under their own policies.
