FAQ

Clear answers.
No fine-print maze.

For legal details, see the Privacy Policy. For help, contact support@owemate.net.

Is OweMate a subscription?

No. Premium Lifetime is planned as a US$4.99 one-time purchase. The free experience supports up to 5 active records.

Can two people use the same records?

Premium supports one Shared Space for two people. Both members can access the records and people stored in that shared space.

What is Secure Vault?

Secure Vault is OweMate’s private area for notes and files that are encrypted on the device before upload. It is not intended to be a password manager.

Can I use OweMate in Arabic?

Yes. OweMate supports English and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. OweMate includes an in-app deletion flow, and a public deletion-request route is available from this website.

Where can I download OweMate?

Android closed testing is underway through Google Play. The iOS build is in rollout preparation; the public App Store link will be added when the listing is live.
